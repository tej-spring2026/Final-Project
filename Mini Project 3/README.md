# Can I Make It? — Boston MBTA Subway Trip Planner

A Flask web application that tells you exactly when to leave so you arrive at your destination on time — using real-time MBTA subway data.

---

## What It Does

Enter where you're starting, where you're going, and when you need to arrive. The app calculates:

- How long it takes to **walk to your nearest T stop**
- **Which train to take** and how long to wait based on live predictions
- How long the **subway ride** takes
- How long to **walk from the T stop to your destination**

Then it tells you: **"Leave by 3:47 PM."**


## 'How to use' Instruction

1. Copy the following URL and paste it in your browser:
- [Application URL](https://can-i-make-it-calculator.onrender.com/)

2. Enter your inputs for starting location, ending location, and the time you want to reach your destination

3. Click the 'Can I make it?' button
